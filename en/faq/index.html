Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Get in touch and we'll answer.

What exactly does Sourty do?
You type a description of the photo you have in mind, in Egyptian Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic or English, and Sourty searches the photos on your phone and shows the closest matches. It also reads the text written inside photos, so you can search for a word or number from a bill, a transfer or a screenshot.
Which phones does it run on?
Android phones with Android 10 or newer. There's no iPhone version right now.
Is it free?
Yes, Sourty is free, with no ads.
Do my photos leave my phone?
No. All photo understanding happens on your phone, and your photos never leave it. There's no account and no sign-up. The details are in the privacy policy.
Does searching need the internet?
No. Sourty prepares and searches your photos on your phone without internet, and doesn't use your data plan.
Will Sourty delete or change my photos?
Sourty won't delete or change anything without your permission.
Does it read Arabic text in photos?
Yes. It reads Arabic and English, and both Arabic and Western digits. You don't have to spell a word exactly as it's written: small Arabic spelling differences are fine.
Does the first run take a while?
Yes. After you install it, Sourty needs time to prepare your photos; how long depends on how many photos you have and on your phone. It works in the background while you use your phone as usual, and you can already search the photos that are ready. After that, every new photo is prepared on its own.
Will it drain my battery?
While it prepares your photos the first time, it works gently when your phone isn't charging and faster when it is. If the battery gets low or battery saver turns on, it pauses and continues when you charge.
When is it coming out?
We're still getting it ready. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know as soon as it's on Google Play.
