Privacy Policy

Sourty (صورتي) for Android · Last updated: November 19, 2026

اقرا بالعربي

In short:

  • Sourty understands your photos on your phone. Your photos, and anything about them, never leave it.
  • The app does not request the internet permission, and has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no trackers.
  • We do not collect or share any data about you.
  • Sourty won't delete or change anything without your permission.

1. Who we are

Sourty (صورتي) is an Android app for searching your photos, developed by Hisham Moawad. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it does with it, and how you stay in control. For any privacy question, contact us at hisham.apps.eg@gmail.com.

2. What the app accesses

  • Your photos: through Android's photo permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13 or newer, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions). On Android 14 or newer you can share selected photos only; Sourty then searches the photos you selected and cannot reach the others.
  • Notifications: to show progress while it prepares your photos, and to tell you when it's done. You can refuse, and the app keeps working.
  • Background work: so it can keep preparing your photos while you use your phone, with a notification shown while it prepares (if notifications are allowed), and it resumes on its own after the phone restarts.

The app does not request the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or video permissions.

3. What it does with your photos

To let you search by description, Sourty does the following on your phone, without sending anything to anyone:

  • It understands the content of each photo and stores the result as numbers that describe it.
  • It reads Arabic and English text written in the photo and stores it so you can search for it.
  • It stores basic details about each photo: its ID on the phone, its dates, its type (camera photo or screenshot), the folder it is in, its file size and dimensions, and, for screenshots, which app they were taken in. It does not store where the photo was taken (location).
  • It keeps your recent searches so you can repeat them quickly. You can clear them in Settings.
  • It keeps your settings inside the app (such as light or dark mode and notifications).

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android locks to Sourty; no other app can read it. Android's automatic backup is turned off for the app, so this data is not copied to any cloud.

4. No data leaves your phone

The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive, sell or share any data about you or your photos.

5. Nothing is deleted or changed without your permission

Sourty reads your photos in order to search them. It does not delete, edit or move any photo, and it will never do so without you confirming it yourself.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • The data Sourty stores stays on your phone for as long as the app is installed, and is not copied anywhere else by Android's backup and transfer tools.
  • When you delete a photo from your phone (while Sourty has access to all photos), Sourty removes its data as soon as it notices the photo is gone.
  • You can delete all of Sourty's data at any time: Android Settings → Apps → Sourty → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can revoke the photo permission at any time in Android Settings.

Since we receive no data, we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Children

Sourty is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

8. Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that changes anything above, we will update this policy and its "Last updated" date before that update is released, and let you know inside the app.

9. This website, sign-ups and feedback

This section is about this website (sourty-app.github.io), not the app.

  • The site has no trackers, no analytics and no cookies, and serves its own fonts and images.
  • If you pick light or dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers may log IP addresses to protect the service, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Waitlist and testing: if you sign up, we store what you entered (your name, your choice to try the app now or be told when it's out, your WhatsApp number or email, your phone model, and your answer about the photo you look for) in a spreadsheet in our Google account.
  • We use your WhatsApp number or email only to tell you when Sourty is released or, if you chose to try it, to add you as a tester. To add you as a tester, we enter your email in the testers list in Google Play Console, so Google lets you download the test version, and we send you the test link at that email.
  • We use your other answers to learn what people need. We won't give your details to anyone else or send you ads. We delete them within 3 months of the app's release, or as soon as you ask (and if you're a tester, we also remove your email from the testers list) at hisham.apps.eg@gmail.com.
  • Feedback form: if you send us feedback from the feedback page, we store what you entered (the type of message, what you wrote, your phone model, and the app version and your email if you entered them) in a spreadsheet in our Google account. The form has no photo upload. We use what you wrote only to improve the app, and your email only to reply to you if you entered it. We won't give it to anyone, and we delete it as soon as you ask at hisham.apps.eg@gmail.com.
